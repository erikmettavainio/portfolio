ALKIMIA

(Single, 28 April 2026)

Fixed media piece written for 16 channels.

Among large FM bells, birdsong, microscopic percussion, zither, modular synthesis and complex clusters of distorted vocals, Alkimia is an imagined soundtracking to the protoscientific alchemical attempts to obtain gold from common earth metals in medieval times.
