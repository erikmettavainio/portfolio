SANERING

(Soundtrack and Audio Design)

“Erik Mettävainios diskanta musik blir det molande i pjäsen som tillsammans med silverfiskarna tycks ta sig fram mellan springor i golvet” (Kult Magasin)

Soundtrack for the original drama ‘Sanering’ written by Walter Hornfelt: a satirical story about the collective and the private, filth and responsibility.

Recorded and realised using the Buchla 200e and SuperCollider systems, guitar, harmonium, percussion and field recordings.

CREDITS:

‘Sanering’ written by Walter Hornfelt

Performed by Teater Tunga at Esperantosceenen, Gothenburg, 2026
Directed by Niels Nevrin Stangertz

Composed, recorded and mixed by Erik Mettävainio at the Royal College of Music in Stockholm, 2025–2026

Sound Operation by Alexander Rydberg

Artwork, illustration and cover design by Elliott Magnusson

‘Sanering’ produced with culture grant from Göteborgs stad

Thanks to Annelie Nederberg
